Founded on Patient-First Dentistry

Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Emily Carter

DDS • Cosmetic & General Dentistry

Dr. Carter leads the SmileCraft clinical team with a calm, detail-oriented approach to general and cosmetic dentistry. She trained in modern restorative techniques and has spent over a decade helping nervous patients rebuild confidence in their smiles — and in visiting the dentist. Outside the clinic, she mentors younger dentists and continues her education in the latest minimally invasive treatment methods.
